THE STORY

Weathered by Sun, Sand and Salt Spray

Every boat has a story. Imagine one that rode the high waves in her prime, guided by a ship’s wheel and an oar, her rest secured by an anchor. Time has weathered her wood, softened her edges and left behind a beauty no new thing can possess.

303 Oyster Bay was born from this love of things that have lived.

Inspired by Iddya’s fishing village, we brought the fisherman’s world home. The living-room wall evokes the weathered walls of a fisherman’s dwelling, setting the scene. Sea-weathered logs have found a second life as a table, their grain and scars bearing memories of the boats they came from. Ropes, Anchor and Ship Wheel that once secured boats to shore and a surfboard shaped by waves and turbulence carry the spirit of journeys past.

Our travels added distant shores to the story. Shells from Rameshwaram, memories of Caribbean beachside cafés and bars, and the unhurried rhythm of life by the sea found their way into this home. Even the simplest pleasures — like slipping on beach slippers and wandering towards the water — belong to this way of life.

The colours, too, recall the ocean: natural sand beige, aquamarine, turquoise, teal and deep ocean blue, flowing through the rooms like the changing tides.

Different objects. Different journeys. One shared soul. A little of Iddya, a little of the Caribbean, beneath the skies of Surathkal.

Welcome to 303 Oyster Bay — where every weathered thing has a story, and the sea brings them together.
